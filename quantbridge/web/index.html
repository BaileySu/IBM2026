Data sources and calculation rules

Holdings use iShares reported market values for USD equities. Weights exclude cash and other assets. The historical price sample covers ten common stocks. SPY supplies the observed trading calendar.

Yahoo split- and dividend-adjusted closes supply simple daily returns. Annualized security volatility is the sample standard deviation of those returns multiplied by √252. A missing required price blocks the affected result. The dataset is a retrospective snapshot.
